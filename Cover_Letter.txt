Dear Editor,

We submit for consideration in Physical Review D the manuscript
"MEML 75: Derivation of the gauge coupling, suppression factor, and
axion decay constant from the geometry of the T^2/Z_3 orbifold"
by D. V. Yurov.

Summary of key results:

1. We derive the gauge coupling alpha_G = 2/(3 pi) = 0.212207 from
   gaugino condensation of a hidden SU(3) group with one flavor of
   matter (N_f = 1, b_0 = 8). Agreement: 0.44%.

2. We derive the suppression factor epsilon = 2.860924 x 10^-4 from
   explicit Kaluza-Klein spectrum summation. The series converges
   rapidly with r_n ~ 1.7 x 10^-3.

3. Main novelty: we derive for the first time the axion decay constant
   from first principles, f_a = M_Pl^red * epsilon^2 = 1.993 x 10^11 GeV,
   using the standard reduced Planck mass M_Pl^red = 2.435 x 10^18 GeV.
   This eliminates the phenomenological normalization present in earlier
   versions.

4. We predict an axion mass m_a = 28.22 ueV and a resonance frequency
   nu_a = 6.825 GHz, accessible to high-frequency haloscopes ADMX-HF,
   HAYSTAC, and CAPP.

5. We obtain the 6D fundamental scale M_6 = 2.965 x 10^16 GeV,
   naturally coinciding with the GUT scale.

6. We provide a rigorous astrophysical module: superradiant instability
   targets sub-Earth-mass primordial black holes (M_BH ~ 0.63 M_Earth,
   R_g ~ 5.59 mm). The Hawking axion channel is kinematically CLOSED
   (T_H = 2.807 ueV < m_a = 28.22 ueV), so spin-down occurs
   exclusively through superradiance with tau_SR ~ 0.854 us.
